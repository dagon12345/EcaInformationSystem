ECA-InFORMS User Manual

A step-by-step guide to every part of the system, organized the same way as the sidebar — so if you can see it in the app, you can find it here. New to the system? Start with Getting Started & Login below, then work through each section in order. Already know what you're looking for? Use the menu on the left to jump straight there.

Tip: press Ctrl/Cmd+F to search this page for a keyword.

Start Here

Getting Started & Login /login

Anyone with an account

The login page is also the system's public landing page — even before you sign in, you can browse the public Feed, a calendar of activities, and quick links to About, Contact (Regional Offices), and Officials (Directory of Officials).

To sign in
  1. Enter your Username and Password, then click Sign in.
  2. If your account has two-factor authentication (2FA) turned on, you'll be asked for a 6-digit code from your authenticator app — enter it and click Verify.
  3. If you enter the wrong password, the page shows how many attempts you have left once you're down to 3 or fewer. After 5 failed attempts your account locks temporarily and a countdown timer appears.
Don't have an account yet
  1. Click Register next to the sign-in button.
  2. Fill in your Full Name, Position, Birth Date, Region, Username, and Password, then submit.
  3. Your account is created in a pending state — you can't log in yet. An Admin/SuperAdmin/Finance user must approve it first under User Management.
Forgot your password
  1. Click Forgot? next to the sign-in button.
  2. Enter your username and submit the request.
  3. This is not an automatic email reset — an admin has to review your request and hand you a one-time reset code (see User Management → Password Resets).
  4. Once you have that code, use the "Reset your password" link to enter it and set a new password.
Tip: If you're locked out repeatedly or your request seems stuck, contact your regional PDO or an Admin directly rather than re-submitting the form.

Understanding Your Role

What you can see and do throughout the app depends on your assigned role. Your role and a live checklist of what you personally can/can't do is always visible at the bottom of the sidebar — click your name/badge there to expand the Your Permissions panel.

The roles in this system
  1. SuperAdmin — full access to everything, including account management and system-wide settings.
  2. Admin — manages grantee records, reports, tracking, and most day-to-day operations for their region.
  3. PDO (Project Development Officer) — works grantee records and directories within their assigned municipalities (jurisdiction); can also log new Application Tracking batches.
  4. Encoder — an office-only role that can create, edit, and view grantee records, with no calling/directory features.
  5. Finance — manages WFP-ECA budgets, user accounts, and attendance/DTR administration.
  6. Viewer — read-only access to most reports and records, plus permission to log new Application Tracking batches.
  7. Focal — a partner-LGU contact with a stripped-down view: Feed (view/comment only), Provincial Focals directory, and their own Call Logs.

See the full Permission Matrix at the bottom of this guide for an exact action-by-action breakdown.

Provincial Focal Accounts /focal-home

Focal role only

A Focal account (a partner-LGU contact invited through Provincial Focals) doesn't use the full staff interface at all — it gets its own simplified layout with just five pages, reached from its own sidebar:

What a Focal account can do
  1. Provincial Directory — the same Provincial Focals directory staff use, for finding people to call.
  2. Feed — the same team Feed as everyone else, but view/react/comment only (no composing posts).
  3. Grantee List — a read-only list of grantee records relevant to their assigned area.
  4. My Call Log — history of their own voice calls.
  5. My Profile — their own account details.

The Messages and Voice Calls tools described below still work exactly the same way for Focal accounts — they appear in the top bar next to the Logout button.

Note: If you're a staff member and land on this simplified view by mistake, it means your account's role is set to Focal — contact an Admin to correct it.

Always Available

These four tools live in the top navbar (or as floating buttons) and follow you to every page — you don't need to navigate anywhere special to use them.

Messages (Chat)

Everyone, including Focal accounts

Direct messaging between staff — click the Messages button in the top navbar (it shows a badge when you have unread messages).

Steps
  1. Click Messages to open the panel and see your conversation list.
  2. Click the compose icon to start a new conversation — search for a person by name and click them to open a chat.
  3. Type your message and send; a green dot next to someone's name means they're currently online.
  4. Click the @@ mentions icon to jump to any message where someone mentioned you.
  5. To remove a conversation from your list, use its delete option and confirm — this can't be undone.
Tip: Inside a one-on-one conversation, a phone icon in the header lets you start a voice call with that person directly — it's only enabled while they're online.

Voice Calls

Everyone, including Focal accounts

Browser-to-browser voice calling between two staff members — no phone number needed, just an internet connection. Calls are started from within a Messages conversation, but can be answered from anywhere in the app.

Making a call
  1. Open a direct conversation with the person in Messages.
  2. Click the phone icon in the conversation header (only enabled while they're online and not already on a call).
  3. Wait for them to accept — you'll see a "Calling…" bar with a cancel button.
Receiving a call
  1. An incoming call shows as a pop-up from anywhere in the app — click the green phone icon to accept, or the red icon to reject.
During a call
  1. A control bar shows the call timer and a live microphone-level meter so you can confirm you're being heard.
  2. Click the microphone icon to mute/unmute, or the red phone icon to hang up.
  3. After hanging up, you can optionally jot down a quick note about what was discussed — this gets saved to Call Logs. Skipping is fine; the call is already recorded either way.

Sticky Notes

All staff roles (not shown to Focal)

Your own private, personal notes/to-do list — click the Sticky Notes button in the top navbar. The same notes also appear in a panel on the Feed page; both are just two entry points to the same list.

Steps
  1. Click + to create a new note, or click any existing note to edit it.
  2. Use the search box to filter your notes.
  3. A note can contain plain text, bullet lines, or checklist lines — click a checklist item directly in the list to check it off without opening the note.
  4. To delete a note, click the trash icon, then confirm.
Note: These notes are private to you — no one else on the team can see them.

Notification Bell

All staff roles (not shown to Focal)

One bell icon in the top navbar covers every kind of alert in the system — click it to open a dropdown with separate tabs, each badged with its own pending count:

Tabs
  1. Duplicates — results from duplicate-grantee scans.
  2. Calendar — today's scheduled activities.
  3. Uploads — background upload/import progress.
  4. Documents — Application Tracking batches currently tagged to you.
  5. Document Tracking — tracked documents currently tagged to you.
  6. Updates — new System Updates you haven't seen yet.
Note: Focal accounts don't have a notification bell — a system update instead shows up for them as a one-time pop-up with a Refresh Now button.

Main

Feed /

Everyone — Focal accounts can view/react/comment only

The team's social wall — announcements, photos, and discussion — plus two built-in team-culture features: a weekly Transaction Leaderboard that auto-posts "Season Champions" and a Birthday widget that auto-posts a greeting for anyone celebrating that day.

Posting
  1. Click the composer box at the top of the feed.
  2. Type your update and/or click the photo icon to attach images.
  3. Click Post. (Disabled until you've added text or a photo.)
Reacting & commenting
  1. Click the reaction button on any post to open the picker: 👍 Like, 😮 Wow, ❤️ Love, 🎉 Celebrate.
  2. Click Comment to open the thread, type your reply, and press Enter or click send.
  3. You can edit or delete only your own posts/comments — look for the pencil/trash icons.
Photos
  1. Click any photo in a post to open it full-screen; zoom/pan and use the arrows to browse multiple images.
Note: Deleting a post or comment shows a confirmation first — this can't be undone once confirmed.

Calendar /calendar

Everyone views; only Admin/SuperAdmin manage, and only for their own region

A month-view calendar of scheduled activities and deadlines across NCSC Caraga, so everyone can see what's coming up.

Browsing
  1. Use Today and the ‹ › arrows to move between months, or click the month title for a quick year/month picker.
  2. Click any day to open a popover listing that day's activities; click the eye icon on one to see full details.
Creating or editing an activity (Admin/SuperAdmin only)
  1. Click + New Activity at the top, or + Add Activity for this day inside a day's popover.
  2. Fill in the details and save. Use the pencil icon to edit, or the trash icon (with a second confirm click) to delete.
Note: You can only manage activities in your own region — an activity from another region shows as view-only, even for Admins.

About / Regional Offices / Directory of Officials

Everyone, including anonymous visitors on the login page

Three informational pages sourced from NCSC's official website: About (the agency's mandate, vision, mission, and core values), Regional Offices (every NCSC office's address, email, and phone number nationwide, with Caraga highlighted), and Directory of Officials (Commissioners, Central Office division heads, and every Regional Director). These are read-only reference pages — nothing to configure, just browse.

Reports

Statistics & Region Map /statistics

All roles

The main dashboard for grantee counts, payment status, and regional distribution — the go-to page for monitoring program progress.

Steps
  1. Expand the filter panel and set Region / Province / Municipality / Payment Status / Payroll Quarter / Fiscal Year as needed.
  2. Click Apply to load the report — summary cards, charts, and municipality/province breakdowns appear below.
  3. Click Refresh any time to reload with the latest data.
  4. (Admin/SuperAdmin) Expand Annual Grantee Target to set a monthly target per region and compare it against actual paid grantees.
Tip: Payroll Quarter and Fiscal Year work together — if you filter by quarter, always also pick a fiscal year, otherwise the count spans every year that quarter ever occurred in.

Applications & Validations /statistics/applications-validations

All roles

A focused report showing how many applications were endorsed vs. validated per LGU — a branch of Statistics with its own, simpler filter set.

Steps
  1. Expand Filters and set a Date Added range, Date Endorsed range, and/or Milestone Year.
  2. Click Apply (or Clear to reset), then review the LGU breakdown table.
Note: This page has its own filters, separate from the main Statistics page — Region/Province/Municipality selections there don't carry over here.

Records

Create Record /beneficiary-create

Admin, PDO, Encoder

Where a new grantee record gets added to the system — either one at a time through the full application form, or many at once via Excel import.

Adding one grantee manually
  1. Scroll to the Manual Entry Form section.
  2. Fill in each section in order: Applicant Info → Address → Family Information → Transaction Account (bank details) → (if applicable) For Deceased Grantees → Attestation/Certification → Verification Checklist.
  3. Click Save at the bottom.
  4. If a similar record already exists, a duplicate-warning modal appears before the save completes — review it carefully before continuing.
Tip: If your session is interrupted mid-form, reopening the page shows a "Restore Draft" banner so you don't lose your work.

Records (Grid & Search) /beneficiary-records

Everyone views/filters/exports; edit actions are gated per-action

The main searchable table of every grantee record — where you look someone up, review their full file, record payments, and generate documents.

Searching & filtering
  1. Use the quick Search anything bar for a name, batch code, barangay, or even a status word like "paid".
  2. Click More Filters to open the advanced filter popup — narrow by Location (Region → Province → Municipality → Barangay), Date Added/Endorsed, Payment Status, Payroll Quarter, Fiscal Year, Mode of Payment, Payment Date, Age & Birthday, Reference Number, or Personal details.
  3. Click Apply Filters, or press Enter anywhere in the popup to apply immediately — Clear resets everything and reopens the popup so you can start over.
  4. Once applied, a row of small tags reading "Filters applied:" appears under the search bar, listing exactly which advanced filters are active — so you don't have to reopen the popup just to check.
Viewing a grantee's full record
  1. Click a row (or its Details button) to pin/highlight it, or double-click a row to open the full record straight away in a side panel — includes Payment History, printable Annex A/B/M/N forms, the Liveness Check preview, and a change-log ("Logs") tab.
  2. Use the panel's Edit button to update the record, or Delete if you have permission.
Bulk actions
  1. Check the boxes next to the rows you want to act on — this enables the toolbar buttons above the table.
  2. Generate COE / Generate Payroll produce documents for the selected grantees.
  3. Export Excel downloads the current filtered list.
  4. Crossmatch Grantees scans for duplicate/possible-duplicate records and flags them with a badge.
  5. The Bulk Update bar has four tabs — Payment, Eligibility/Batch, CO Status, and Ref Number — fill in the relevant fields and click the tab's Apply button to update every selected row at once.
Note: Nothing in the toolbar or bulk-update bar works until you've checked at least one row — the page reminds you of this right in the header.
Auto-eligibility updates (turning 80)
  1. Under R.A. 11982, a grantee becomes Eligible automatically once they actually reach age 80 and their citizenship is Filipino — the system watches for this among whatever records are currently loaded (no separate scan needed).
  2. When one or more such grantees are found, an Auto-Eligibility Update popup appears listing their Name, Birth Date, Age, Citizenship, and Current Eligibility so you can double-check before anything changes.
  3. Use View next to any row to scroll to and highlight that grantee in the grid below without losing your current search results.
  4. Click Yes, Mark Eligible to confirm — this updates the record(s) and adds an entry to that grantee's Logs tab noting it was an automatic update. Click Not Now (or just close the popup) to decide later; nothing is changed until you confirm.
  5. A green "N Auto-Eligibility Pending" tag appears next to the record count whenever there's a pending list waiting — click it anytime to bring the same popup back, even after closing it.
  6. Once confirmed, the updated row is highlighted green in the grid for the rest of your session as a visual confirmation.
Note (PDO accounts): A PDO only ever sees auto-eligibility candidates that fall inside their own assigned jurisdiction — anything outside it never appears in the popup in the first place, so every row shown can always be confirmed. Admin, SuperAdmin, and Encoder accounts have no jurisdiction restriction and see every candidate.

Bulk Import via Excel

Admin, PDO, Encoder

Found at the top of the Create Record page — lets you add many grantee records at once from a spreadsheet instead of the manual form.

Steps
  1. Click Download Template to get the correctly formatted spreadsheet.
  2. Fill it in, then upload the file and choose the correct worksheet.
  3. Optionally set a Reference Number prefix (quarter/batch/year) — a live preview shows exactly how numbers will be generated.
  4. Click Run Preview to validate every row before anything is saved.
  5. Fix any row-level errors shown inline — some offer one-click suggestions, a location picker, or digit-only corrections you can undo individually.
  6. If possible duplicates are found, review them in the popup: toggle each row to Skip or Import, or use Skip All / Import All Anyway.
  7. Confirm to run the final import.
Tip: Nothing is saved until you confirm — Preview is a safe, repeatable dry run.

Files

Forms Gateway /forms-gateway

Everyone views/downloads; only Admin/SuperAdmin upload, edit, or delete

The shared library of NCSC form templates and payroll-linked documents, organized into folders.

Steps
  1. Browse folders in the left sidebar, or use the search bar / sort toggle at the top.
  2. Click Preview or Download on any form card.
  3. (Admin/SuperAdmin) Click New Folder to organize, or Upload Form to add a document — give it a title, choose the file, and optionally tag it to a Payroll Quarter/Fiscal Year.
  4. Use the pencil/trash icons on a form card to edit or delete it.
Note: Linking an uploaded PDF to a payroll quarter/year is what makes it automatically show up as that grantee's payroll document in the Records page's Payment History — don't skip this if it's a payroll form.

Financial

Liquidation (CDR) /liquidation

Admin only

Generates the Cash Disbursements Record (CDR) — an Excel report of paid grantees grouped by municipality and milestone year, used for financial liquidation.

Steps
  1. Use the filters (province, payment date) to load paid records.
  2. Review the grouped preview — each group shows its grantee count and total disbursement.
  3. Click Generate CDR to produce the Excel file.
Note: Generate CDR stays disabled until records are loaded. If a banner warns that more records match than are shown, narrow your filter before generating.

WFP-ECA /wfp-eca

Admin/Finance/SuperAdmin can view; only Finance/SuperAdmin can edit

The region's Work Financial Plan — a UACS budget line-item table (Allotment / Obligation / Balance) tracked per fiscal year.

Steps
  1. Pick a Fiscal Year from the dropdown.
  2. (Finance/SuperAdmin) Click Add Line Item, fill in UACS Name, UACS Code, Allotment, Obligation, and Remarks, then save (check icon) or cancel (x icon).
  3. Reorder rows with the up/down arrows; delete a row with the trash icon (requires a confirm click).
Note: Every add, edit, delete, or reorder saves immediately — there's no separate "Save all" step.

Tracking

Application Tracking /application-tracking

Everyone views; logging a batch is Viewer/PDO/Admin; only the current holder can move it forward

Tracks physical/scanned document batches (grouped by province, municipality, milestone year, and date received) as they pass through a fixed chain of custody, from initial endorsement to completion.

The workflow
  1. Endorsed by Viewer or PDO → Returned to whoever logged it → Distributed to PDO → Endorsed to Finance
  2. (loop as needed) Returned to PDO with Findings ⇄ Forwarded to Viewer — Scanning
  3. Completed
Steps
  1. (Viewer/PDO/Admin) Click New Application Batch, fill in Province/Municipality/Milestone Year/Date Received, list the grantee names, and tag who receives it.
  2. Click a batch card to expand it; use the search box or status dropdown to find one quickly.
  3. Whoever is currently tagged as the holder sees action buttons appropriate to that stage (e.g. Endorse to Finance, Forward to Viewer — Scanning, Mark Completed) — click one and confirm. The "Return to..." button always names whoever actually logged the batch (Viewer or PDO).
  4. (Admin/SuperAdmin) Can correct a wrongly-tagged recipient without changing the stage. SuperAdmin can edit or delete any batch regardless of who currently holds it.
Tip: Click "How does this work?" at the top of the page any time for a quick in-page reminder of the stage order.

Document Tracking /document-tracking

Anyone can create; deleting is Admin/Finance/SuperAdmin

The free-form sibling of Application Tracking — for relaying a single document person-to-person, with no fixed stages.

Steps
  1. Click New Tracked Document, give it a title (a serial number is generated automatically), and tag the first recipient.
  2. Use the search bar to find a document by serial number or title.
  3. Click View on a document to relay it to someone else, return it (the system picks the recipient automatically), or mark it complete.
  4. If a document is tagged to you and awaiting your acceptance, click Accept.

Directory

Senior Citizens Directory /senior-citizen-directory

Everyone views; PDO/Admin/SuperAdmin edit

A shared, region-scoped reference directory of senior citizens — separate from grantee records, useful for outreach and LGU contact information (LSWDO, SC Focal, OSCA Head, FSCAP President, Mayor, and more).

Steps
  1. (Editable roles) Click Add to create one entry, or Import Excel to bulk-load many — download the template, upload your filled-in file, pick the worksheet, run a preview, resolve any flagged duplicates, then confirm.
  2. Use View and History on any row to see full details or its change log — available to everyone.
  3. Use Edit/Delete if you have permission.
Tip: A green "Live" badge means changes from other users appear automatically, with no need to refresh.

NCSC Team Directory /ncsc-team-directory

Everyone views; only Admin/SuperAdmin add, edit, delete, or import

A nationwide contact directory of NCSC field and regional office staff — grouped by region, not just Caraga.

Steps
  1. Browse entries grouped by region, or use the search/region filter at the top.
  2. (Admin/SuperAdmin) Click Add Entry for one person, or Import Excel for many.
  3. For import: download the template, fill it in (or download the official NCSC roster directly and upload it as-is — the system recognizes both formats automatically), select the worksheet, validate, then confirm.
  4. Use the History, Edit, and Delete icons on each card as needed.

Provincial Focals /provincial-focals

Everyone except Encoder; inviting is PDO/Admin/SuperAdmin

Directory of PDOs and their partner-LGU "Focal" contacts, grouped by province — with click-to-call for anyone currently online. This is also how a Focal account gets created in the first place.

Steps
  1. Click How it works for a quick explainer.
  2. (PDO/Admin/SuperAdmin) Click Invite Focal, select the province/municipality context, fill in the focal's details, and click Create Invite — then Copy the generated link and send it to them.
  3. Use Pending Invites to review outstanding invitations or generate a fresh link if the old one wasn't used.
Important: The invite link is not saved anywhere once you close the dialog. If you forgot to copy it, go to Pending Invites and click "Get Link" to generate a new one.

Call Logs /call-logs

SuperAdmin/Admin see everyone's calls; PDO sees only their own

A history of every voice call made through the app's built-in browser-to-browser calling feature — who called whom, when, how long, and any notes attached.

Steps
  1. Browse the table — it's read-only for everyone except SuperAdmin, who can delete an entry if needed.

Administration

Attendance Management /attendance-management

SuperAdmin, Finance

Links each staff account to their biometric (fingerprint) device ID so Daily Time Records can be generated automatically, and lets you review or print anyone's DTR or a month-wide attendance summary.

Steps
  1. Assign tab: search for a user, enter their Biometric ID, and click Save.
  2. Summary tab: pick a period (1-15, 16-End, Full Month, or Custom), click Generate, then View DTR on any user for their printable record.
  3. Device tab: enter the biometric device's IP/port, Save Settings, Test Connection, or Run Local Sync to pull the latest punches.
Note: Syncing punches from the device only works while connected to the office network — but reviewing already-synced DTRs works from anywhere.

User Management /user-management

SuperAdmin, Finance

Central account administration — approving registrations, assigning roles and jurisdictions, handling password-reset requests, and deactivating or deleting accounts.

Users tab
  1. Switch between role sub-tabs (e.g. Pending, Active, Deactivated) and use the filter panel to narrow by name/region/province/municipality.
  2. Approve or Reject a pending registration.
  3. Use Reassign role to change someone's role, or Set Jurisdiction to choose which municipalities a PDO can act on (multi-select, with "Select All Visible").
  4. Reactivate/Deactivate an account, Reset MFA if someone's locked out of their authenticator, or Delete to permanently remove an account.
Password Resets tab
  1. Review incoming requests, then Approve or Reject each one.
  2. Click View Code to see the one-time reset code to hand to the user, or Regenerate Code if it expired.
Warning: Deleting a user is permanent and cannot be undone — deactivating is the safer, reversible option for someone who's just left temporarily.

Conversation Oversight /chat-oversight

SuperAdmin only

A read-only audit tool for reviewing direct-message conversations between users, for compliance purposes.

Steps
  1. Search for a conversation by name/username in the left panel.
  2. Click a conversation to load its messages.
Note: Opening a conversation here is itself recorded in the Activity Logs for accountability.

System Updates /system-updates

SuperAdmin only

Publish release notes that push live, in real time, to everyone currently using the app.

Steps
  1. Fill in Version (auto-suggested, editable), Title, and Changes (one line per change).
  2. Click Publish & Notify Everyone.
  3. Review past releases in the Update History panel.

Activity Logs /logs

Admin, SuperAdmin

The audit trail of actions taken on grantee records — payments, bulk updates, edits — for accountability.

Steps
  1. Expand Filters and set a date range, username, beneficiary name, and/or a keyword to search within the activity text.
  2. Click Apply and page through the results.

Your Account

Profile & Security /profile

Everyone (your own account)

Manage your avatar, personal details, two-factor authentication, and active login sessions. Visiting someone else's profile shows their public info instead (name, position, region, tier).

Steps
  1. Click your avatar to view it full-size, or Remove picture to clear it.
  2. Edit your details and click Save.
  3. If 2FA is off, click Set Up MFA — see Two-Factor Authentication below.
  4. Under Active Sessions, click Revoke on any device you don't recognize, or Log out all other devices to sign out everywhere else at once.

Two-Factor Authentication /mfa-setup

Everyone

Adds a second layer of login security using an authenticator app (like Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator) alongside your password.

Steps
  1. Scan the QR code shown on the page with your authenticator app, or click Copy to enter the code manually.
  2. Enter the 6-digit code your app generates.
  3. Click Enable Two-Factor Authentication.
Note: You can click "Set up later" to skip for now — you'll be reminded again at your next login. To turn 2FA back off later, go to Profile and confirm with your password.

Daily Accomplishment Report /dar

Every staff user, for their own reports

Semi-monthly accomplishment reporting — log what you worked on each day of a pay period, then print the finished report.

Steps
  1. Click New Report to start a new period, or Open an existing one from the list.
  2. Click the pencil icon to edit the header fields, then Save.
  3. For each daily entry row, click its pencil icon to edit inline, then the check icon to save that row (or the x icon to cancel).
  4. Scroll down for a live Print Preview of the finished report.
Note: There's no single "save everything" button — each row saves itself as soon as you confirm it.

My DTR /my-dtr

Every staff user, for their own record

Self-service view of your Daily Time Record, built from your biometric device punches.

Steps
  1. Choose the Year/Month and a period preset (1-15, 16-End, Full Month, or Custom).
  2. Click Pull Latest Record.
  3. Click any time cell to add, edit, or clear a punch by hand — useful when a device punch was missed or wrong. Saturdays and Sundays work the same way now: click the pencil icon next to "SATURDAY"/"SUNDAY" to add an entry for a day office activity happened; a blank weekend keeps showing the default label until you do.
Note: If you see "No biometric device is linked to your account yet," ask a SuperAdmin or Finance user to assign your Biometric ID under Attendance Management. Punches only sync when someone runs the sync tool on the office network — but you can view your DTR from anywhere afterward.
